This is the abstract template for the 22$^\mathrm{st}$ International Congress on Plasma Physics, to be held in Hyderabad, India. The final submission must be sent in PDF format. 

Your abstract must be written using Times or Times New Roman font. Abstract title must be centered, 1.5 spacing and 14pt bold with initial letters capitalized. Authors, affiliations and body text must be 12pt font. For body text, use single spacing and justified margins. The page size is A4 (21 cm x 29.7 cm). Page margins are 2.54 cm from the top, left, right and bottom. The abstract must be in English and the length is limited to \textbf{one page} (including title, authors, affiliations, body text, references, acknowledgements, etc.) and \textbf{400 words at most}. If your abstract includes references, they must be made in brackets around the number, i.e.~\cite{interestingpaper}, at the end of the document~\cite{anotherpaper}.

The abstract must be submitted in \textbf{PDF format} using the abstract submission utility at the conference website. Abstracts submitted in the body of an email \textbf{will not be accepted}. Also, please remember to delete this text before submission.
